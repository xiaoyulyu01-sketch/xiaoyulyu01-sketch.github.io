Isolation, Cultivation, and Screening of Halo-Alkalitolerant Microorganisms from Natural Saline Soils

Targeting extremophilic bacteria thriving in harsh saline-alkali environments across Northwest China. Utilizing selective media subjected to salinity (NaCl) and pH gradient challenges, this project isolates robust bacterial strains capable of tolerating dual halo-alkaline stress, establishing axenic cultures and cryopreserved strain biobanks for potential plant growth promotion (PGPR) in arid agriculture.

Habitat Origin: Northwest China Saline Soils Stress Screening: Dual Salinity (NaCl) & Alkaline pH Gradients Preservation: -80°C Glycerol Stock Cryopreservation

Core Experimental Workflows Culturomics & Phenotyping

1. Field Soil Sampling

Conducted field campaigns collecting soil cores and rhizosphere samples from naturally occurring saline-alkali terrains in Northwest China. Measured baseline soil EC, pH, and exchangeable sodium percentage (ESP).

2. Dual Stress-Gradient Plating

Formulated specialized selective media incorporating escalating NaCl concentrations (up to 15%) and elevated alkaline pH conditions (pH 8.5–10.5). Isolated survival-tolerant colonies through spread-plate dilution series.

3. Pure Culture & Cryopreservation

Achieved axenic pure isolates via repeated four-quadrant streak plating under laminar flow hoods. Documented colony morphology, Gram reactions, and established long-term -80°C glycerol cryo-collections.
