Nutrient Turnover & Batch Colorimetric Optimization

Optimized 96-well colorimetric microplate reader protocols for high-throughput batch quantification of soil mineral nitrogen (ammonium-N and nitrate-N) and available phosphorus (Olsen-P). Investigated subterranean C/N/P stoichiometry and water-nutrient synergies governing overyielding in drylands.
