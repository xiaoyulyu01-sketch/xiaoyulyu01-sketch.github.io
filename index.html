About Me

I am a final-year Master's candidate in Ecology (expected graduation in June 2027) at the College of Ecology, Lanzhou University (兰州大学). My empirical research investigates belowground resource facilitation mechanisms, root-zone soil moisture depletion, and plant–soil phosphorus partitioning in rainfed cereal–legume intercropping systems across semi-arid Loess Plateau drylands.

Prior to graduate studies at Lanzhou University, I received my Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.) in Landscape Architecture from Northeast Forestry University (东北林业大学) in 2024, acquiring solid training in ecological site design, soil physical fundamentals, and geospatial modeling.

1. Membrane Lipid Substitution ($P_L \to \text{SQDG/DGDG}$) Replacing membrane phospholipids with sulfolipids and galactolipids maintains $P_i$ and $P_M$ above critical thresholds, sustaining net photosynthesis ($A$) and maximizing PPUE under P deficiency.
2. Phosphorus Resorption Efficiency (PRE) High-PUE species remobilize up to ~70% of foliar P during senescence, where $P_i$ (63%) and $P_L$ (16%) govern the export gradient, mitigating soil nutrient exhaustion in drylands.

Manuscript in Final Preparation 2026

Coordinated leaf phosphorus allocation and membrane lipid remodeling enhance photosynthetic phosphorus-use efficiency in dryland crops

Xiaoyu Lyu, et al.

Target Journal: Agriculture, Ecosystems & Environment (Submission Draft)

Related Figure 7
Abstract: Crop adaptations to low phosphorus in semi-arid drylands depend on how plants partition limited P among subcellular fractions and reorganize cellular membranes. This study investigates the ontogenetic dynamics of leaf inorganic-P ($P_i$), metabolic-P ($P_M$), lipid-P ($P_L$), and nucleic acid-P ($P_N$) across contrasting PUE crop regimes. Results reveal that targeted sulfolipid and galactolipid substitution for membrane phospholipids preserves $P_i$ and $P_M$ pools above critical thresholds, mitigating photosynthetic depression and enhancing photosynthetic phosphorus-use efficiency (PPUE) across vegetative growth.

Depth-stratified soil moisture facilitation and phosphorus fractions dynamics under semi-arid cereal–legume cropping systems

Xiaoyu Lyu, et al.

Target Journal: Field Crops Research / Plant and Soil

Trial Details
Abstract: Cereal–legume intercropping has been widely recognized for sustainable intensification in water-limited drylands, yet depth-stratified interactions between root-zone water depletion and phosphorus fractions turnover remain incompletely resolved. We conducted multi-depth (0–100 cm) soil moisture profiling and high-throughput 96-well colorimetric determinations of soil mineral nitrogen and Olsen-P across maize intercropped with companion legumes (lablab bean and forage soybean). Linear mixed-effects modeling showed that complementary root architecture mitigated drought penalties in upper layers while facilitating phosphorus mobilization in deeper horizons.
