Mechanistic Insights & Theoretical Basis

Phosphorus Plasticity & Photosynthetic Coupling

Crop adaptations to low-phosphorus drylands depend fundamentally on how plants allocate limited P among cellular compartments (vacuoles, chloroplasts, cytosol, nuclei) and mobilize bound nutrient reserves prior to leaf abscission:

Membrane Lipid Substitution ($P_L \to \text{SQDG/DGDG}$)

Replacing membrane phospholipids with sulfolipids and galactolipids preserves inorganic ($P_i$) and metabolic ($P_M$) pools above critical thresholds, maintaining net photosynthesis ($A$) and maximizing PPUE.

Phosphorus Resorption Efficiency (PRE)

High-PUE species remobilize up to ~70% of foliar P during senescence, where $P_i$ (63%) and $P_L$ (16%) govern the resorption gradient, mitigating soil nutrient exhaustion.

Translation to Intercropping Facilitation

Combining deep-rooted companion legumes with high-PPUE cereals exploits complementary rhizosphere P mobilization and moisture niches in rainfed drylands.

Field Trial & Agronomy 2025.04 – Present

Dryland Rainfed Maize–Legume Intercropping & Moisture Dynamics

Graduate Research Assistant · Lanzhou University

Experimental Setup: Designed and operated large-scale rainfed plot trials on the semi-arid Loess Plateau evaluating maize intercropped with companion legumes (lablab bean Lablab purpureus and forage soybean Glycine max) in randomized complete block and split-plot layouts.

In-Situ Soil Moisture Monitoring: Calibrated sensor probes and deployed time-domain reflectometry to record depth-stratified soil moisture (0–100 cm) across key phenological stages under natural precipitation deficits.

Facilitation vs Competition: Evaluated root spatial segregation, canopy light interception, and crop water productivity, identifying optimal row configurations that balance overyielding benefits against water depletion penalties.

Analytical Wet Lab 2025.04 – Present

High-Throughput Microplate Colorimetry for Mineral N & Olsen-P

Batch Chemical Profiling & Nutrient Stoichiometry

Analytical Instrumentation: Adapted and optimized 96-well colorimetric microplate spectrophotometry protocols for simultaneous batch quantification of soil ammonium-N ($NH_4^+-N$), nitrate-N ($NO_3^--N$), and available phosphorus (Olsen-P).

QA/QC & Matrix Correction: Established 8-point linear calibration curves achieving $R^2 > 0.999$, implemented internal spike-recovery checks (95–104%), and suppressed matrix optical interferences in saline-alkali extracts.

Nutrient Turnover: Processed 700+ dryland core samples to determine depth-resolved C:N:P stoichiometry and water–nutrient coupling governing intercrop nutrient uptake.

Microbiology & GIS 2025.10 – Present

Arid PGPR Isolation & Regional Agroecological Spatial Modeling

Saline-Alkali Gradient Studies & Niche Modeling

Microbiological Screening: Sampled soils along natural salinity-alkalinity gradients in Northwest China; conducted aseptic streak-plate isolation, colony phenotypic characterization, and cryopreservation of halo-alkalitolerant bacteria.

MaxEnt Niche Models: Coupled regional occurrence records with high-resolution WorldClim bioclimatic and ISRIC soil grids in MaxEnt to predict climatic suitability boundaries and environmental thresholds under aridification scenarios.

Spatial Zoning in QGIS: Created raster suitability overlays and topographic slope/aspect classifications in QGIS to guide regional dryland crop allocation.
